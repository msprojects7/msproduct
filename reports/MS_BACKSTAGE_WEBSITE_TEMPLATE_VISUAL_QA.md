## 2. Visual QA Verification Results

- **Typography & Font Hierarchy:** Headings render with correct font families and high contrast across both light and dark mode backgrounds.
- **Hero Banner Readability:** Fixed high-contrast text rendering on hero banners with drop shadows, ensuring headline and subtitle readability regardless of background image luminosity.
- **Device Responsiveness:** Tested and verified across Desktop (1440px), Tablet (768px), and Mobile (390px) viewports with zero horizontal overflow.
- **Interactive Component Fidelity:** Ticket cards, speaker modal triggers, multi-track agenda tabs, and contact forms respond immediately with accessible focus rings and hover transitions.
